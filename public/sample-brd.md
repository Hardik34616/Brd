# Farmer Connect — Sample Business Requirements Document

Illustrative Bhasha BRD landing-page example. Not generated by live AI.

## Executive summary
Help farmers make informed crop decisions using local weather information and crop guidance.

## Business objectives
- Make relevant weather information accessible to farmers.
- Define clear requirements before development starts.

## Stakeholders
Farmers, agronomists, product owners, and platform administrators.

## Functional requirements
- FR-001: Farmers can view soil moisture information when a supported data source is available.
- FR-002: Opted-in farmers receive location-based weather alerts through their selected channels.
- FR-003: Farmers can submit crop photos for review; diagnosis workflow requires validation.

## Non-functional requirements to clarify
Agree on language coverage, accessibility targets, data retention, availability, and response times with stakeholders.

## User story
As a farmer, I want local weather alerts so that I can take action to protect my crops.

## Acceptance criteria
Given an opted-in farmer with a saved location, when a severe weather event applies to that location, the system sends one relevant notification through the selected channel.

## Open questions
- Which regions and languages are included in the first release?
- Which weather and soil data sources will be used?
- Who reviews crop photos, and what are the limits of advice?
- What budget, delivery date, and success measures have stakeholders agreed to?
